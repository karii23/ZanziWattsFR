06 · Orientation & sun path

Face north. Tilt at 8.6°–10°.

Zanzibar sits below the equator, so the array faces north toward the sun’s dominant path. A shallow tilt balances annual yield, wind loading and row-to-row shading.

07 · Fieldwork archive

Behind the Scenes Real Data collected: Direct from IIT Madras Zanzibar

Open the field notes behind the model. Every assumption traces back to a campus observation, measurement or operating record.

79 units were counted: 23 on the campus right side, 6 serving the cafeteria and matron, 3 at the clinic, and 47 across classrooms, offices and labs. Nominal capacity: 1.25 ton each.
Five zones total 3,501 m²: 921.19, 562.73, 764.36, 627.36 and 575.66 m². The design reserves 2,150 m² after access and tilt clearances.
During semester-break months with no classes, the campus consumes approximately 15,000 kWh/month. This is treated as a seasonal dip—not normal operation.
June 29,040 kWh · July 20,400 kWh · August 15,920 kWh · September 14,560 kWh. October operating target: 1,200 kWh/day.
N-Type TOPCon monocrystalline modules, adjustable from 580–670 W, were selected for high efficiency, low degradation and better performance in hot conditions.
